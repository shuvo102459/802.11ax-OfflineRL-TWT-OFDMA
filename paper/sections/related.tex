\section{Related Work}

\textbf{TWT scheduling.} TWT has been studied for IoT power
saving~\cite{chen2019twt}, category-aware grouping~\cite{qiu2021category},
latency-sensitive traffic~\cite{yang2021twt}; recent work treats the schedule itself as
the object of optimisation~\cite{busacca2026twt}. Karaca~\cite{karaca2020joint}
optimises the TWT mechanism jointly with scheduling, and Roy et
al.~\cite{roy2023maximization} maximise timely throughput under TWT, allocating
OFDMA resources \emph{within} an awake group. Both fix a policy by analysis or optimisation. We fit one to logged data
instead.

\textbf{Uplink OFDMA scheduling.} A separate line schedules OFDMA without TWT:
deep RL for the OFDMA MAC~\cite{kotagiri2021distributed}, coexisting latency
classes~\cite{tan2024drl}, hierarchical RL for joint uplink OFDMA and
MU-MIMO~\cite{noh2024joint}, multi-agent RL for uplink
OFDMA~\cite{han2024multiagent,liu2025priority}. Objectives are mostly
throughput or delay, though
some include power~\cite{liu2025priority}; what none makes part of the control
problem is station sleep. Both mechanisms do appear
together~\cite{karaca2020joint,roy2023maximization}; what is absent is a
station-energy account in which the OFDMA decision is controlled per decision
rather than assumed available, and in which sleeping more competes in the same
action space.

\textbf{Machine learning for Wi-Fi and radio resource management.} Online RL
has been applied to contention-window control~\cite{wydmanski2021contention} and
to the problems above, with live-network exploration the obstacle to deployment.
Offline RL~\cite{levine2020offline} removes it and pays in distribution shift,
addressed by conservative value estimation~\cite{kumar2020cql,kostrikov2022iql};
its wireless applications concentrate on cellular resource
management~\cite{yang2024offline,eldeeb2026marl}, where
action spaces are modest. We combine offline learning with per-decision control
of the scheduler and score it on station energy. Maksud and Carvalho~\cite{maksud2026drltwt}, coauthors
here, apply \emph{online} deep RL to dynamic TWT scheduling and share the ns-3
scenario and energy accounting; this paper adds the OFDMA bit and the grouping
to the action, learns offline, and asks whether the result beats a tuned
hand-designed controller.
