\subsection{Seeds and protocol}

Three disjoint seed sets are used, so no policy is evaluated on data that
informed it: collection from $30000$, tuning on $80000$--$80014$, and all
reported results on $90000$--$90049$.

The fifty evaluation seeds are \emph{paired}: a seed fixes the traffic, the
time of any load change and its multiplier, so every policy meets an identical
environment. Pairing reduces the variance from those shared conditions, which
lets fifty seeds resolve differences that would otherwise need many more.
Energy is reported over the $42$-decision window, excluding the warm-up.

\subsection{Baselines}

Nine non-learned scenarios span the configuration space, from no power save to
a tuned hand-designed controller (Table~\ref{tab:main}).

The primary adaptive comparator is a hysteresis rule that raises the duty cycle
when the observed backlog proxy crosses an upper threshold and lowers it below a
lower one. Its thresholds, schedule pair and OFDMA mode come from a staged
coordinate search on the tuning seeds, minimising energy subject to a service
floor: mean throughput at least $95\,\%$ of the highest any candidate in that
sweep achieves, so it cannot win by refusing traffic. The static baseline is chosen the same way.
